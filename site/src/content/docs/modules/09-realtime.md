title: "Module 9: Realtime delivery"
description: Choose WebSockets or SSE from the interaction model.
---

WebSockets are bidirectional and support collaborative commands. SSE is a one-way, browser-friendly stream over HTTP with automatic reconnection. Define event names and payload versions, send heartbeats, remove disconnected clients, and bound queues so a slow consumer cannot exhaust memory. Reconnect with backoff and treat duplicate events as normal.

Complete [Lab 09](/labs/09-realtime/) and try both clients.
