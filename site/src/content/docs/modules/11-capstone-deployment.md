The capstone combines JWT identity, project-scoped roles, durable repositories, cursor pagination, signed imports, and operational probes. Define a threat model, migration and rollback plan, contract tests, smoke checks, and an incident runbook. Deployment is successful only when secrets are external, migrations precede traffic, readiness gates dependencies, and retries do not duplicate work.

Use [Lab 11](/labs/11-capstone-deployment/) and the PostgreSQL Compose rehearsal from Lab 07.
